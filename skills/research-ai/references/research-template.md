# Research Template

This document provides a structured template for research outputs produced by the research-ai skill.

## Document Structure

### Introduction
Brief overview of the research topic and its significance.

### Literature Review/Findings
Depends on research level:
- **Academic**: Scholarly evidence, peer-reviewed studies, academic databases
- **Professional**: Industry reports, expert opinions, professional resources
- **General**: News sources, public information, general knowledge

### Key Insights
Summarized findings relevant to the research topic.

### Conclusion
Summary of main points with implications for future research or application.

## APA Citation Format

All sources must use proper American Psychological Association (APA) format including:
- In-text citations
- Reference list at the end
- Appropriate formatting by source type

## Output Formats

This research can be delivered in multiple formats:

1. **PowerPoint (.pptx)** - For presentations with slides
2. **Word (.docx)** - For detailed reports and documents  
3. **PDF** - For printable documents
4. **Markdown (.md)** - For plain text with formatting
5. **Excel (.xlsx)** - For data tables and spreadsheets
